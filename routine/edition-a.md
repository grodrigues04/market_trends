Edition A (complete edition, Thursday). No limit on the number of subagents or pages.

The only limit is the usage of the 5-hour window: when it reaches 87%, the system denies every new
search, page and subagent (`LIMIT REACHED`). From then on, use the rest of the session only to
finish what is in progress and write the part with what you have. The countries not started yet
are skipped by the system, and the conclusions are written from the countries that are ready.

- Country sessions: as many research subagents as the niches need (for example 5 researchers with
  5 niches each, plus 1 for local platforms, viral content and creators). One additional round of
  research at most, only for critical gaps.
- Conclusions session: write parts 7 to 11 with writer subagents that read all the notes and the
  six country parts. Parts 8, 9, 10 and 11 can be written by separate writers in parallel after
  part 7 exists. Spawn extra research subagents only for real gaps (the Brazilian market check of
  the international transfer analysis usually needs one). If the 87% limit was already reached,
  no subagent can be spawned: write parts 7 to 11 yourself, without new research.
